Text

Uses the Text & logo style toggle above -- Inlay keeps the whole face flush for a clean face-down print with no supports, Emboss raises the letters proud of the surface for a face-up print instead. Works alongside any shape, including an imported logo. The font choices here favor thick, even strokes -- serif and script fonts tend to fill in or break off at small sizes, so they're left out.
